## Grow an audience

| Idea | What to do | Learn | Where |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Affiliate marketing | Promote products for a commission. No stock, no shipping. | [Affiliate basics (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jv6MBZTwqUc) | [Amazon Associates](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com) · [Impact](https://impact.com) |
| Faceless YouTube | Compilations, tutorials or animations | [Channel ideas (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GyX1l3CO0rM) | [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com) |
| Newsletter | Niche email newsletter, free to start | [Substack guide (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JSqAKsL3ZtU) | [Substack](https://substack.com) · [Ghost](https://ghost.org) |
| Podcast | Faceless podcast on free hosting, earn from ads | [Start a podcast free (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VsmUnTgLzFQ) | [Spotify for Podcasters](https://podcasters.spotify.com) · [Anchor.fm](https://anchor.fm) |
| Theme pages | Niche Instagram or X pages, earn from affiliate links | [Theme page growth (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dt9gJ7DaYJg) | [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com) · [X](https://twitter.com) |

Most of these need no money upfront, only skills, time and consistency. Affiliate marketing, faceless YouTube and blogging can start at ₹0.

## Tips

- Pick a niche. Stay with it until it pays.
- Build a portfolio: GitHub, a personal site, Behance.
- Network on Reddit, Discord and LinkedIn groups.
- Put in 1–2 hours every day.
