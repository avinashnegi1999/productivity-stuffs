## Principles

1. Estimate income conservatively, from real market salaries.
2. Aim for 15–20% yearly returns through disciplined investing.
3. Save 40–70% the whole way.
4. Build 3–4 income streams. Never rely on one.
5. Put 10% of income into skills and knowledge.
6. Manage risk: emergency fund, insurance, conservative estimates.

Keep saving as income grows. Give compounding 10+ years. Adapt to the market. Go deep in 1–2 skills. Build relationships in tech.

## Risks

| Risk | Defense |
|:--|:--|
| A downturn cuts tech salaries | Keep 12–18 months of expenses saved |
| A health emergency drains savings | Full health and life insurance |
| The SaaS doesn't find its market | Plan on 70–80% success scenarios, keep a Plan B career and income |
| Property prices outrun the plan | Review quarterly and correct course |
