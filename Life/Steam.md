## How XP works

| Action | Reward |
|:--|:--|
| 1 badge craft | 100 XP |
| Full game badge, level 5 | 500 XP |
| Every 10 Steam levels | 1 more profile showcase |

## Cost to level 50

In India. About 12,000 XP, or about 120 badge crafts.

| Approach | Per badge | Total |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Smart farming, recommended | ₹25–30 | ₹3,000–3,600 |
| Optimized: sell backgrounds and emoticons, craft event badges in sales | | ₹2,200–2,800 |
| Lazy: buy cards anytime, no resale | | ₹4,000–5,000 |

## Tips

- Buy trading cards, not games.
- Focus on 5-card badge sets.
- Craft each badge up to level 5.
- Sell backgrounds and emoticons.
- Farm event badges during Steam sales.
- Reinvest your wallet balance.

**Level 50 gets you:** 5 showcase slots, more booster pack drops, a bigger friends list, a credible profile.
