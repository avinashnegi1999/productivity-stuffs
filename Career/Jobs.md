# Jobs

Remote, English-language jobs with international companies. For backend and full-stack developers aiming at the US, the EU and beyond.

## How to apply

- **Every week:** Arc.dev, Turing, We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Remotive, Wellfound.
- **With email alerts:** Hired, Working Nomads, Jobspresso, Jobgether, FlexJobs, Remote.co.
- **Freelance while you hunt:** Upwork, Contra, PeoplePerHour. Builds reputation.
- **Daily:** Check the internship lists. Apply within 48 hours.
- **Weekly:** Pick companies from the GitHub lists. Apply on their careers pages.

Target backend and Python roles at remote-first companies. Track every application. Consistency beats volume.

## Tips

- **Profile first.** On Arc.dev, Turing and Hired, a strong profile brings companies to you.
- **Use the "Worldwide" filter** on Remote OK and similar boards. Many listings block regions otherwise.
- **State your overlap.** IST (UTC+5:30) overlaps EU mornings and US afternoons.
- **Get paid easily.** Set up Wise or Payoneer before you apply.
- **Know your number.** Backend Python developers with 2+ years and strong DSA earn $30K–80K remotely. Negotiate in USD.
- **GitHub is your cover letter.** Many remote companies skip letters and read your code. Keep it clean and active.

**Research:** salaries on [levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi) and [Glassdoor](https://glassdoor.com) · payments with [Wise](https://wise.com) and [Payoneer](https://payoneer.com)
