# Blender

Build better 3DXChat worlds. 6 weeks.

Only the Blender skills that carry over to the 3DXChat World Editor.

## Weeks 1–2: Move in 3D

- Orbit, pan, zoom.
- Move `G`, rotate `R`, scale `S`.
- Duplicate and arrange cubes like LEGO.

**Learn:** [Blender Beginner Tutorial (Blender Guru)](https://youtu.be/TPrnSACiTJ4) · [Blender Basics in 20 Minutes](https://youtu.be/LLV7h-WLIxE)

## Weeks 2–3: Build with blocks

- Vertices, edges, faces.
- Extrude `E`, inset `I`, loop cut `Ctrl+R`.
- Make walls, floors, stairs.
- Array and Mirror modifiers for repeats and symmetry.

**Learn:** [Modeling Basics](https://youtu.be/m9AT7H4GGrA) · [Array Modifier Explained](https://youtu.be/6OTR9yYjNT0)

## Weeks 3–4: Materials and light

- Simple materials: color, roughness, metallic.
- Point, area and sun lights.
- Mood lighting: club scene vs beach scene.

**Learn:** [Materials Tutorial](https://youtu.be/jtU1g7uJA1Q) · [Lighting Tutorial](https://youtu.be/gk7t0SjkYts)

## Weeks 4–5: Lay out a scene

- Block out a house with cubes.
- Tile floors and walls with the Array modifier.
- Organize with Collections.
- Place cameras to plan how people see the space.

**Learn:** [Blocking Out Environments](https://youtu.be/fTPuS6M3thY) · [Modular Environment Design](https://youtu.be/jOTgJ4EKi4g)

## Weeks 5–6: Build in 3DXChat

- Build with modular primitives.
- Copy and paste the way Array and Mirror work.
- Recreate your Blender lighting with 3DXChat lamps.
- Build a simple room based on your Blender scene.

## Optional: Automate

- Geometry Nodes for grids, forests and repeating structures.
- Export layouts to the 3DXChat XML format.

**Learn:** [Geometry Nodes Beginner Tutorial](https://youtu.be/mS9jF6FzBRQ)
