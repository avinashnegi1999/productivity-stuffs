## 📋 Notes & Strategy

### 🧭 Recommended Approach for International Developers

**Tier 1 — Apply actively every week:**
Arc.dev → Turing → We Work Remotely → Remote OK → Remotive → Wellfound

**Tier 2 — Set up email alerts and apply when relevant:**
Hired → Working Nomads → Jobspresso → Jobgether → FlexJobs → Remote.co

**Tier 3 — Freelance to build reputation while hunting:**
Upwork → Contra → PeoplePerHour

---

### 💡 Tips That Actually Help

- **Profile > Applications** — On Arc.dev, Turing, and Hired, a strong profile means companies approach you. Optimize these first.
- **"Worldwide" filter** — Always use this on Remote OK and similar boards. Many listings block certain regions unless you filter.
- **Timezone alignment** — Even for remote roles, mention UTC+5:30 (IST) overlap with EU mornings and US afternoons. It reassures employers.
- **Payment setup** — Wise or Payoneer are essential before applying to international roles. Have your account ready.
- **USD/EUR salary benchmarks** — Backend Python devs with 2+ years and strong DSA typically earn $30K–$80K remotely. Negotiate in USD.
- **GitHub + portfolio = trust** — Many remote companies skip cover letters and go straight to your GitHub. Keep it clean and active.
- **Crossover caution** ⚠️ — Research their time-tracking and monitoring practices (screenshot-based) before committing.
- **Toptal** ⚠️ — Accepts ~3% of applicants. Only apply after significant real-world project experience.
